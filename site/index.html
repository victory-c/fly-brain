Learning, attempt by attempt · new

The fly learns to ride

Starting from a blank decoder that cannot steer, the fly's whole brain learns to ride a Colnago V4Rs down a 7 m road at 20 km/h in side gusts, by trial and error: 1,680 attempts in 35 generations of 48, every one recorded. In generation 0, 42 of 48 fall within a second; by the last, 29 of 48 ride the full 15 s. Watch him fall, drift into the grass and set new records one attempt at a time, with his brain firing in the side panel. The connectome never changes; only the 91 numbers between his descending neurons and the handlebars do.

What is real

Neurons, synapses and their signs come from the Janelia / Google male CNS v1.0 connectome; the neuron model follows Shiu et al., Nature 2024. How a drink or a bike's lean maps onto sensory neurons is modeled, and so is the bike decoder.

Where it ran

On a Slurm node with RTX A6000 GPUs. One whole-brain trial takes ~8 s per simulated second; batched on the GPU, 128 trials take 35 s. In the bike loop, 48 riders run at ~20 s of wall time per second of bike time.
